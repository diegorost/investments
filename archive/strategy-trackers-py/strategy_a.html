Strategy Tracker

Three normal actions. CONSIDER BUYING = the underlying is inside this ETF's buying zone and no sell is pending; it does not mean a fresh unused rung appeared. Buy any amount ($200, $500, $2,000, other) or nothing, and buy again at the same rung if you choose. SELL ALL = the underlying's daily close reached the sell reference and tracked shares are positive; it takes priority. WAIT = no actionable normal signal. CHECK DATA is a data warning, never a trade signal.
  • DROP FROM ATH is the underlying's drawdown (close vs its intraday all-time high), not the leveraged ETF's performance.
  • LEVEL REACHED, LOWEST UNUSED LEVEL and NEW LEVELS READY are information about where price sits in the ladder, not purchase gates. New levels ready = 0 does not prohibit buying.
  • Shares, cash and sale value come only from what you record in TRADES after a confirmed broker fill.

Each rung is a percentage below the underlying's ATH, with the underlying price that would trigger it. BUY = reached and not yet used this cycle. USED = a recorded BUY at that rung in the current cycle; it counts history and does not block another discretionary buy. WAIT = not reached. A cycle restarts only when a SELL takes tracked shares to zero; a new month or a new monthly sell reference does not reset the ladder.

How this tracker decides

  1. Drawdown = underlying daily close / underlying intraday all-time high (split-adjusted, dividends not adjusted) − 1. Shown negative when below the ATH.
  2. Buying zone is active while the drawdown is at least the ETF's first tier. Rungs step 5 points deeper (up to 16 rungs, below 100%). Reaching a rung is information; repeat purchases at any reached rung, in any size including zero, are allowed.
  3. Sell reference: Strategy A = the underlying's all-time intraday high before the latest session (the pre-drawdown ATH). Strategy B = the underlying's highest intraday price in the previous completed calendar month, advanced each month.
  4. SELL ALL when the underlying's daily close is at or above the sell reference and tracked shares are positive. It depends on the underlying, not on whether the ETF recovered its cost: actual proceeds can be below cost.
  5. Action priority: CHECK DATA (invalid or missing prices) > SELL ALL > CONSIDER BUYING > WAIT. A disabled or paused pair always shows WAIT.
  6. Cycle key = ticker + cycle number; the number advances when a SELL takes tracked shares to zero. Used rungs and "new levels ready" count within the current cycle. A new ATH is the economic reset of the ladder; the tracker's cycle key follows recorded sells, so the two can differ.
  7. Tracked cash = starting cash + cash flows + sells (gross − fees) − buys (gross + fees). Tracked shares, cash and sale values include only what is recorded here; broker holdings not recorded (for example historical MUU shares) are outside the tracker.
  8. Data quality: a quote older than the last completed US session is flagged stale (weekday rule; holidays are not known to the page). Never act on a label while data are stale or invalid.

Daily workflow

  1. After the close, run python refresh.py and reload both pages.
  2. On START HERE check the quote dates and DROP FROM ATH, then WHAT TO DO.
  3. CONSIDER BUYING: decide the amount yourself (including $0); the ladder columns are optional context. SELL ALL: verify the completed session, the sell reference and actual broker holdings first.
  4. Execute with the broker, then record the confirmed fill in TRADES. Confirm shares and cash moved as expected.
  5. Monthly: confirm Strategy B's previous-month-high sell references advanced; review total exposure, actual cash and leveraged-ETF decay risk.

Decision support only. These pages do not connect to a broker, execute trades, or guarantee prices or profits. Leveraged ETFs reset daily and can lose value even when the underlying recovers. Not financial advice.
